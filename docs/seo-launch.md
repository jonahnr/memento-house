# Memento House SEO launch checklist

The application now supplies canonical metadata, Organization and product structured data, public guide pages, `robots.txt`, and a complete XML sitemap at `https://mementohouse.com/sitemap.xml`.

## Search Console

1. Create a Google Search Console Domain property for `mementohouse.com` and verify it with the DNS TXT record Google provides.
2. Add the verification token (only the token value) to Vercel as `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` for Production and Preview, then redeploy.
3. Submit `https://mementohouse.com/sitemap.xml`.
4. Inspect the home page and each product page, request indexing once, and monitor indexing, Core Web Vitals, HTTPS, and structured-data reports weekly during launch.
5. Keep `mementohouse.com` as the only canonical origin. Redirect `www` to the apex domain and never index preview deployments.

## Content and internal links

- Publish one useful planning guide at a time; answer a real buyer question rather than repeating product copy.
- Link every guide to the most relevant product and celebration page, and link product pages back to supporting guides.
- Extend celebration pages only when the offer and images are ready; do not create thin near-duplicate pages.
- Add descriptive image alt text, original photography, author/reviewer information, and a visible last-reviewed date where helpful.

## Authority and measurement

- Earn relevant links through local wedding vendors, planners, venues, photographers, directories, and real product features rather than buying links.
- Ask partners to link to the closest product or guide page, not always the home page.
- Connect privacy-respecting analytics and record checkout-start, checkout-complete, demo-use, and contact conversions.
- Review Search Console queries monthly and improve pages that receive impressions but weak click-through or engagement.

## Before legal launch

Have qualified counsel confirm the legal operator name, complete postal address, governing-law language, privacy retention practices, tax obligations, and consumer-rights disclosures. Replace the current location-level operator identification if a company entity is formed.
